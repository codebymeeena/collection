Zoro Card Collection

Every English One Piece TCG Zoro card — use + and − to count the ones you have.

Site version 4

All prices are shown in Canadian dollars, converted from the US-dollar prices below at the weekly exchange rate. The big price on each card is the last eBay sale; if there is none, it falls back to the eBay sold average, then TCGplayer. (Collectr has no public prices, so it is linked on each card instead.) Prices: TCGplayer market price (via tcgcsv.com). “Sold (raw)” and the recent-sales list come from PriceCharting, which tracks eBay sold listings for ungraded English copies. Each card links to eBay sold listings, TCGplayer, Collectr and PriceCharting so you can double-check. Your quantities are saved in this browser — use Export to back them up.
